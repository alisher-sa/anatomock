1. Medical & Educational Disclaimer

Strictly for Academic & Educational Use Only

1.1. Not Medical Advice: AnatoMock is a free, student-oriented educational platform designed solely to help university students practice anatomical structure identification and prepare for academic spotter examinations (OSPE). Nothing on this website—including specimen photographs, anatomical plates, needle placements, accepted answers, or revision notes—constitutes medical advice, clinical diagnosis, prognosis, treatment, or professional healthcare instruction.

1.2. No Physician–Patient Relationship: Accessing or using AnatoMock does not create a physician–patient, clinical, or professional advisory relationship between the platform operators and any user. Never disregard, avoid, or delay obtaining medical advice from a licensed physician because of information read on this website.

1.3. Ethical Use of Anatomical & Cadaveric Imagery: Photographs of real human osteological specimens, cadaveric dissections, and classical anatomical illustrations (such as public-domain Gray's Anatomy plates and cited medical repositories) are displayed strictly for non-commercial university instruction. Users must treat all anatomical donor imagery with academic respect and may not download, redistribute, or use these images outside of personal study.

1.4. No Guarantee of Academic Accuracy: While accepted answers follow standard English and Latin Terminologia Anatomica (Nomina Anatomica), anatomical variations and curriculum differences exist across universities. AnatoMock makes no warranty regarding the completeness or accuracy of any question and is not responsible for individual university examination results.

2. KVKK (Law No. 6698) & GDPR Privacy Policy

Clarification Text on the Processing of Personal Data (Aydınlatma Metni)

In accordance with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), AnatoMock acts as the Data Controller (Veri Sorumlusu) and processes only the minimum data necessary for your use of the platform.

2.1. Personal Data Collected: When you create a student account or submit a message, we collect only:
• Name & Surname (Identity Data)
• Email Address (Contact Data)
• University Grade / Class (1st Grade, 2nd Grade, or 3rd Grade)
• Study Mistake Log (Questions missed during Exam-Mode so you can review them)
We do not collect national ID numbers, health data, payment/financial data, location data, or third-party advertising cookies.

2.2. Purpose & Legal Basis of Processing: Your data is processed under KVKK Article 5/2 (establishment/performance of a service and legitimate educational interest) and GDPR Article 6(1)(a)–(b) strictly to: (i) authenticate your login session, (ii) direct you to the tests and blocks matching your university grade, (iii) maintain your personal Mistake Log, and (iv) reply to feedback sent through the Contact form.

2.3. Data Storage & Third-Party Sharing: We never sell, rent, or commercially share your personal data with third parties or advertisers. Essential functional data (login session, language selection, and mistake log) is stored solely to operate the application.

2.4. Your Rights & Instant Account Deletion (KVKK Art. 11 / GDPR Art. 17): You have the right to access, correct, or permanently erase your personal data at any time. You can immediately and permanently delete your account and all associated data with a single click using the "Delete Account" button located on the Student and Grade pages, or by contacting us via the Contact section.
